La Traviata

Upcoming

by Giuseppe Verdi

March 15-17, 2024 | Main Theater

Verdi's timeless tale of love and sacrifice tells the story of Violetta, a courtesan who falls in love with Alfredo. When his father demands she leave him for the family's honor, she must make the ultimate sacrifice.

The Magic Flute

Upcoming

by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

June 8-10, 2024 | Main Theater

Mozart's enchanting opera follows Prince Tamino as he embarks on a quest to rescue Pamina, guided by a magic flute. A delightful blend of comedy, fantasy, and Masonic symbolism.

Carmen

Upcoming

by Georges Bizet

October 12-14, 2024 | Main Theater

Bizet's passionate opera tells the story of Carmen, a free-spirited gypsy, and Don José, a soldier whose obsession leads to tragedy. Featuring some of opera's most beloved melodies.
