The Magic Flute

by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart

Dates: June 8-10, 2024

Venue: Main Theater

Duration: Approximately 3 hours (including intermission)

Buy Tickets Learn More

Synopsis

The Magic Flute is Mozart's enchanting fairy tale opera that follows Prince Tamino as he embarks on a quest to rescue Pamina, the daughter of the Queen of the Night. Armed with a magic flute and accompanied by the bird-catcher Papageno, Tamino must pass a series of trials to prove himself worthy of love and enlightenment.

The opera is a delightful blend of comedy, fantasy, and Masonic symbolism, featuring some of Mozart's most beloved melodies including the Queen of the Night's famous aria "Der Hölle Rache" and Papageno's playful "Der Vogelfänger bin ich ja."

Through its magical story and beautiful music, The Magic Flute explores themes of love, wisdom, and the search for truth and enlightenment.
